Practical marketing toolkit

Start with these metrics: conversion rate = conversions ÷ visitors; CAC = acquisition spend ÷ new customers; ROAS = attributed revenue ÷ ad spend; retention = customers who remain over a period.

Try this: choose a product, define one target audience, write a clear value proposition, create a simple campaign brief and specify how you would measure success.

Metrics need consistent definitions and reliable data. Attribution and ROAS can be misleading when channels overlap.

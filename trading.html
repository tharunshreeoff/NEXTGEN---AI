Read a market move carefully

  1. Identify the instrument, exchange and quote currency.
  2. Check the timestamp and whether the market is open.
  3. Look for the catalyst: earnings, economic data, policy, supply or geopolitical news.
  4. Compare the move with volume, volatility and a relevant benchmark.
  5. Separate verified facts from commentary and speculation.

This page is for news and education, not live exchange quotes, trade signals or personalised financial advice. Prices can be delayed. Verify data with your broker or an authorised market-data provider before making decisions.
